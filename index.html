Japanese Sushi Rice with Pan-Fried Salmon
~550 kcal2 servings30 min
Ingredients
  • 300g Japanese short-grain rice
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar, 2 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp salt (sushi seasoning)
  • 200–250g salmon belly or salmon skin pieces
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Spring onion, sesame seeds to serve
Method
  1. Rinse rice until water runs clear. Cook in a rice cooker or pot (1:1.1 rice to water). Rest 10 min.
  2. Mix rice vinegar, sugar, and salt until dissolved. Fold gently into hot rice. Fan to cool — don't refrigerate.
  3. Pat salmon dry. Season lightly with salt and pepper. Heat a non-stick pan on medium-high, no oil needed for belly (it's fatty enough).
  4. Lay salmon skin-side down for 3–4 min until skin is crispy, then flip for 1–2 min. Deglaze with soy sauce and sesame oil right at the end.
  5. Serve salmon over sushi rice, top with spring onion and sesame seeds.
💡 Salmon belly is cheap at most supermarkets and much more forgiving than fillet — hard to overcook. Salmon skin pieces are even cheaper and the crispy skin is the best part.
Pasta Bolognese (Tomato Minced Meat)
~620 kcal2 servings25 min
Ingredients
  • 160g spaghetti or penne
  • 250g minced beef or chicken
  • 1 can (400g) crushed tomatoes
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, dried oregano, chilli flakes
  • Parmesan to serve (optional)
Method
  1. Boil pasta in salted water until al dente. Reserve a cup of pasta water before draining.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan. Fry onion until soft, then garlic for 1 min.
  3. Add minced meat, break it up, cook until browned. Season with salt, pepper, oregano.
  4. Pour in crushed tomatoes. Simmer 10 min until thickened. Taste and adjust seasoning.
  5. Toss in drained pasta, splash pasta water to loosen if needed. Serve with parmesan.
💡 The pasta water is starchy and helps the sauce cling to the pasta — don't skip it. Minced chicken is leaner and cheaper; minced beef gives a richer flavour. Both work well.
Pasta Aglio Olio with Minced Meat
~580 kcal2 servings20 min
Ingredients
  • 160g spaghetti
  • 200g minced chicken or beef
  • 5 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 3 tbsp good olive oil
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes (adjust to taste)
  • Salt, black pepper
  • Handful of fresh parsley or spring onion
Method
  1. Boil pasta in well-salted water until just al dente. Reserve a big cup of pasta water.
  2. In a wide pan, heat olive oil on medium-low. Add garlic — cook slowly until golden (not brown). Add chilli flakes.
  3. Push garlic to the side, turn up heat, add minced meat. Brown well, season.
  4. Add drained pasta directly into the pan. Pour in 3–4 tbsp pasta water. Toss everything together — the starchy water and oil emulsify into a light sauce.
  5. Finish with parsley or spring onion, a crack of black pepper.
💡 Aglio olio lives or dies on two things: good olive oil and properly salted pasta water. Don't rush the garlic — low and slow gets you golden, not bitter.
Simple Egg Fried Rice
~420 kcal2 servings15 min
Ingredients
  • 2 cups day-old cooked rice (cold rice is key)
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Spring onion, white pepper
Method
  1. Beat eggs with a pinch of salt. Get your wok or pan very hot.
  2. Add oil, then garlic — stir 20 seconds. Add rice, press and toss to break up clumps.
  3. Push rice to the side, pour eggs in, scramble until just set, then fold into the rice.
  4. Add soy sauce, sesame oil, white pepper. Toss on high heat 1–2 min.
  5. Off the heat, add spring onion. Serve immediately.
💡 Freshly cooked rice is too wet and clumps — day-old refrigerated rice fries up perfectly. This is the best use of leftover rice.
Miso Soup with Tofu & Wakame
~90 kcal2 servings10 min
Ingredients
  • 500ml dashi or plain water
  • 2 tbsp white or red miso paste
  • 150g silken or firm tofu, cubed
  • 1 tbsp dried wakame seaweed (rehydrated)
  • Spring onion to serve
Method
  1. Rehydrate wakame in cold water for 5 min, then drain.
  2. Heat dashi or water until just about to boil. Add tofu gently.
  3. Turn off heat. Dissolve miso paste in a ladle of the hot liquid first, then stir into the pot. Never boil after adding miso — it kills the flavour.
  4. Add wakame, serve immediately with spring onion.
💡 Miso paste and wakame are available at most Asian supermarkets in Singapore — giant packs last months in the fridge/pantry. This makes an excellent low-calorie side alongside any meal.
Simple Chicken Congee (Porridge)
~350 kcal2 servings40 min
Ingredients
  • 100g uncooked jasmine rice
  • 1L chicken stock or water
  • 200g chicken breast or thigh, sliced thin
  • 2 slices ginger
  • Soy sauce, sesame oil, white pepper
  • Spring onion, fried shallots, century egg (optional)
Method
  1. Bring stock and ginger to a boil. Add rice, stir well.
  2. Reduce heat to a gentle simmer. Cook 25–30 min, stirring occasionally, until rice breaks down into a thick porridge. Add more water if too thick.
  3. Add chicken slices, cook 3–4 min until just cooked through.
  4. Season with soy sauce, sesame oil, white pepper. Serve topped with spring onion and fried shallots.
💡 Congee is one of the most naturally low-calorie filling meals out there — mostly water and a small amount of rice, but incredibly satisfying. Great for days when you've already eaten a lot.
Honey Garlic Chicken
~450 kcal2 servings20 min
Ingredients
  • 400g boneless chicken thigh or breast, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar or apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Steamed rice and spring onion to serve
Method
  1. Mix honey, soy sauce, vinegar and sesame oil into a sauce. Set aside.
  2. Heat vegetable oil in a pan on medium-high. Add chicken, cook 4–5 min until golden.
  3. Add garlic, stir 30 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Pour sauce over chicken. Toss and cook 2–3 min until sauce thickens and coats everything.
  5. Serve over rice with spring onion.
💡 Chicken thigh stays juicier than breast here — its fat bastes the meat as it cooks. If using breast, don't overcook or it'll dry out fast.
Teriyaki Beef Rice Bowl
~580 kcal2 servings20 min
Ingredients
  • 300g beef slices (shabu shabu cut or thin sirloin)
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp mirin or 1 tbsp sugar + 1 tbsp water
  • 1 tbsp sake or water
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Spring onion and sesame seeds to serve
Method
  1. Mix soy sauce, mirin, sake into teriyaki sauce.
  2. Heat oil in a pan on high. Add beef slices in a single layer — don't stir immediately, let them sear 1 min.
  3. Flip, pour teriyaki sauce over, cook another 1–2 min until sauce reduces and caramelises slightly.
  4. Finish with sesame oil off the heat.
  5. Spoon over steamed rice, top with spring onion and sesame seeds.
💡 Thin beef slices (like those sold for shabu shabu or sukiyaki) cook incredibly fast — this whole dish is done in under 5 minutes once your rice is ready. Available in most supermarkets in Singapore.
Lemony Baked Salmon with Vegetables
~420 kcal2 servings25 min
Ingredients
  • 2 salmon fillets (~150g each)
  • 1 lemon, zested and sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp dried herbs (oregano, thyme, or mixed herbs)
  • Salt and pepper
  • Any vegetables — broccoli, cherry tomatoes, zucchini, baby corn
Method
  1. Preheat oven to 200°C. Line a baking tray with foil.
  2. Mix olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, herbs. Rub over salmon and vegetables.
  3. Arrange salmon and vegetables on the tray. Top salmon with lemon slices.
  4. Bake 15–18 min until salmon flakes easily with a fork.
  5. Season, serve immediately.
💡 One pan, zero washing up. You can swap salmon for any firm fish fillet — tilapia and barramundi both work well and are cheaper.
Spiced Lamb Mince with Rice (Keema-style)
~520 kcal2 servings25 min
Ingredients
  • 250g minced lamb or beef
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tomato, diced (or 3 tbsp tomato paste)
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp coriander, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp chilli powder
  • Salt and pepper
  • Fresh coriander leaves
  • Steamed rice to serve
Method
  1. Heat oil in a pan. Fry onion until golden, add garlic 1 min.
  2. Add all spices, stir 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add minced meat, break up and cook until browned.
  4. Add tomato, cook down 5–8 min until mixture is dry and aromatic. Season well.
  5. Serve over rice, topped with fresh coriander.
💡 This spice mix is flexible — go heavy on cumin for more depth, add more chilli for heat. Lamb has a stronger flavour that works really well with these spices; beef is milder. Both are halal-friendly at any certified halal butcher or supermarket halal section.
Grilled Chicken Wrap
~480 kcal2 servings20 min
Ingredients
  • 2 boneless chicken thighs
  • 2 large wraps or flatbreads
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika, 1/2 tsp cumin, salt, pepper
  • Lettuce, sliced tomato, sliced cucumber
  • Plain yogurt or hummus to spread
  • Squeeze of lemon
Method
  1. Mix olive oil with paprika, cumin, salt and pepper. Coat chicken.
  2. Grill or pan-fry chicken on medium-high heat, 5 min each side until cooked through. Rest 2 min, then slice.
  3. Spread yogurt or hummus on the wrap. Layer in chicken, vegetables, squeeze of lemon.
  4. Roll tightly and serve.
💡 These keep well — make extra chicken and the next day's lunch is sorted. Works with leftover rotisserie chicken too.
